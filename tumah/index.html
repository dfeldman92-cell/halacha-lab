The chain follows tumah left → right. “Is touched by” and “touches” both represent direct contact; the node on the left is the tumah source for that step.

🧭 Core map

מגע
Basic direct contact.
משא
Some sources, such as נבילה and zav-family sources, contaminate a person through carrying.
מדרס
Zav-family sources can make qualifying supports אב-level מדרס.
אהל
Corpse-source system; עצם כשעורה is a key exception.
אויר כלי חרס
Earthenware receives through inner airspace rather than ordinary external touch.
משקין
Rambam treats the special onward power of tamei liquids as rabbinic.

Method note

This is a learning simulator, not a complete psak engine. It intentionally flags טומאת מת–כלים and other technical branches instead of flattening them. Food chains assume הכשר unless Precision mode says otherwise. Generic כלי means a completed susceptible vessel. Food→food onward transmission is labeled rabbinic according to Rambam where applicable.
