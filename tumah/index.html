Ohalos 1:1–4
The pattern אדם → אדם gives only two tamei links. כלים can extend the chain. When an אדם is in the middle, the chain can reach four links before dropping to טומאת ערב.

חרב הרי הוא כחללThis game follows Rambam: the special כלי chain is not limited to an actual sword and includes metal vessels, כלי שטף and clothing. Some Rishonim restrict this extension to metal vessels; non-metal extensions get a ⚖️ badge.
